Use `CubeWatermark` to give navy panels and marketing surfaces their texture.

```jsx
<div style={{ position: "relative", overflow: "hidden", background: "var(--navy)" }}>
  <CubeWatermark cube="03" size={340} opacity={0.16} base="../../" />
</div>
```

Always oversized and clipped by the frame edge. .14–.18 opacity inside navy cards; higher only on white marketing layouts where the cube is the image.
