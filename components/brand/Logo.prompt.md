Use `Logo` wherever the brand signs a surface — footers, headers, covers, social.

```jsx
<Logo lockup="oneline-black" height={22} base="../../" />
<Logo lockup="stacked-white" width={280} base="../../" />
```

Green is the default on white; black on light or green fields; white on navy, black and photography. One-line for footers and narrow bands, stacked for covers and social. `base` is the relative path back to the design-system root so the PNG resolves.
