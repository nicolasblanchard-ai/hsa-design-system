Use `AudienceCard` when the same offer has to be told differently to three audiences.

```jsx
<AudienceCard label="For investors" title="Vetted dealflow">
  <Bullet size={13}>First look at 16&ndash;20 companies a year.</Bullet>
</AudienceCard>
```

Always in a row of two or three; the navy headers must line up, so give the row `align-items: stretch`.
