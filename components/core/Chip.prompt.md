Use `Chip` for short qualifiers and figures that ride alongside a heading or card.

```jsx
<Chip tone="value">CHF 15k + up to 200k</Chip>
<Chip tone="outline" marker>Investor-Ready</Chip>
<Chip tone="draft" pill>Draft &middot; to tailor</Chip>
```

`draft` is the only place an unverified figure may appear. `outline` is the cover-slide qualifier with the mint marker bar.
