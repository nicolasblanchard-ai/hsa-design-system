// Logo

Six official lockups.

One-line (≈16.4:1) for footers, headers, narrow bands and email signatures. Stacked (≈3.1:1) for covers, title slides, posters and social. Use the delivered files unchanged — never recolour, stretch, outline or add effects.

Green — default on white and light neutral backgrounds
Black — on light or green backgrounds, and wherever one colour is required
White — on navy, black, photography and solid colour fields
One-line — minimum 22px tall, the deck footer standard
Clear space

// Voice & content

Short declaratives that argue.

Europe innovates a lot.
But Europe has stopped building winners.
Because the pipeline breaks at the lab door.
Sentence case, ending in a period. Consecutive slides chain with connectives — But, Because — set in mint inside the headline.
Audience
DeepTech founders, investors, mentors, experts, ecosystem members.
Voice
Clear, direct, practical, connecting. We are the connectors.
Tonality
Casual, clear, entertaining, positive, youthful, professional.
Language
American English. CHF before the amount, en dash for ranges, 24-hour time.
Do
Short sentences, one to two per line, each ending in a period.
One clear CTA per post or page.
Numerals except at sentence start; four digits and up take an apostrophe — 6'000.
Max three emoji per post or newsletter — never in decks or documents.
Max four hashtags on social: #Acceleratorthroughaccess #DeepTech #InvestorReady #StGallen.
Don't
Long paragraphs, or links buried mid-block.
Emoji stacks, hyphens for ranges, hashtags in newsletters.
Mint as body emphasis — it belongs to the connective word.
Shadows, recoloured lockups, small centered cubes.
Unverified numbers outside a dashed yellow draft tile.
Social post structure
Catchy opening line → brief intro → key points as bullets → CTA → visuals → mentions → closing.
Newsletter
Logo header → short subject line → thematic greeting → labelled sections with bullets → one or two mint CTA buttons → Partner Spotlight → footer with social and unsubscribe. One newsletter, one core message.
